Our Mission & Platform

Revolutionizing Digital Healthcare Access

DocBook is an all-in-one patient healthcare hub designed to eliminate long clinic queues, bridge doctor discovery, provide instant AI triage, and deliver vital medicines right to patients.

Direct Booking

Connect directly with qualified specialists across Cardiology, Dermatology, Neurology, Pediatrics, and General Medicine without middleman overhead.

Intelligent AI Healthbot

Ask health doubts anytime. Our virtual assistant provides immediate clinical guidance, first aid suggestions, and emergency hospital warnings.

GPS Hospital Radar

Instant discovery of certified emergency trauma centers and hospitals with one-click direct telephone calling and routing directions.

Doorstep Pharmacy

Express delivery of certified OTC medications, immunity vitamins, first aid kits, and digital monitoring health devices.

⚡ 100% Frontend Architecture

Zero Backend Dependencies.
Ready for GitHub Pages.

DocBook has been crafted as a completely serverless static web application. All data—including doctor profiles, user registrations, appointment scheduling, and e-cart state—persists securely in the browser's localStorage. You can host this entire platform on GitHub Pages with zero server costs!
